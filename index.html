      About

      QR Shield is a static web app that decodes QR codes with jsQR, identifies what they contain, and runs local URL checks.

      What the local checks can and cannot do

      The checks look for patterns that often appear in phishing links, such as IP-address hosts, embedded credentials, lookalike brand names and unusual encoding. They cannot confirm that a site is safe or malicious. A LOW RISK result only means those patterns were not found.

      Threat intelligence

      No reputation service is connected. A Google Safe Browsing check needs a secure backend that holds the API key. The integration point is ready in script.js, and reports show "Not verified" until it is connected.

      Privacy

      Images, camera frames and URLs stay in your browser. Scan history is saved in this browser's local storage only.
